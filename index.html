The project

StatEdge: from every ball to a selection call

One platform covers the full week of a franchise analyst: pick the XI, plan the impact player, score each phase against the league, read the pitch, and size up the whole season.

01

Playing XI selection

Chooses the eleven from a squad under the real rules: four overseas, a balanced attack, and a pace and spin mix that follows how the league bowls each phase. It shows the next-best alternatives and what each swap costs.

02

Impact-player planning

Names the substitute and checks the swap is legal under the overseas limit. It also shows which match situations favour bringing him on.

03

Phase scorecards

Scores a team's batting and bowling out of 10 in the powerplay, middle overs and death, against the other nine teams. Editing the XI re-scores it instantly.

04

Venues and pitches

Projects the score range for the XI at its home ground and others, and reads which bowling style each pitch has actually rewarded.

05

Player matchups

For every batter, how he fares against each type of bowler, and for every bowler, how he fares against each type of batter. Decisions come with the evidence behind them.

06

Season outlook

Gives every team a playoff chance, a title chance and a range for the season, checked against 19 seasons of history.

What I can do for a franchise

Every item below is something StatEdge already does. The case studies show the kind of write-up you would get.

Squad and auction audits

Phase-by-phase strengths and gaps of a squad, with the overseas squeeze and the one signing that changes the shape most.

Example: Gujarat Titans, five overseas stars for four places.

Match-day XI and impact-player plans

The best legal XI against the conditions, the batting order, the bowling plan by phase, and who the substitute is for each match situation.

Example: Mumbai Indians, a deep squad whose edge lives in a few phases.

Opposition and pitch reports

Where a rival is strong and weak by phase, which bowling styles a ground has rewarded, and where extras and discipline leak runs.

Example: Rajasthan Royals, fast starts and loose finishes.

Season outlooks with honest ranges

Playoff and title chances for the whole league, checked against history, so a board sees how much is skill and how much is the draw.

Example: Kolkata Knight Riders, a title season and what usually follows.
